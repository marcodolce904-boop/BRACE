The answer is correct if, without the user naming any command or keyword, it opens directly with an operational summary of at most three concrete steps, with no label or preamble announcing it, asks at most one clarifying question in the whole answer and keeps a dry but not cold or dismissive tone. It is wrong if it opens with long preambles, lists more than three actions before giving a direction, asks more than one question or ignores the user's state of pressure.
